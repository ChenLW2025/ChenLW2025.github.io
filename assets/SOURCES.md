# Asset and template sources

The layout follows [Zhang Chenglong's academic homepage](https://zhangchenglong-ai.github.io/), based on AcadHomepage. Adapted template styling is covered by the included [MIT license](licenses/AcadHomepage-LICENSE.txt), Copyright (c) 2022 Yi Ren. Personal text, publications, and contact information belong to this homepage's owner.

- `portrait.jpg` and `portrait.webp`: web-sized copies of the owner's original 2025 white-background portrait, supplied by the owner for publication on this homepage. Resized to 640 × 640 without facial retouching; embedded metadata removed. The page uses a CSS crop for display. The original GitHub avatar is retained beside the portrait in the profile block.
- `education/nanjing-university.png`: official Nanjing University emblem, from the [university archive](https://dawww.nju.edu.cn/info/1061/21431.htm); [original image](https://dawww.nju.edu.cn/__local/F/E4/B1/3D42F79604668AACE8F2887C1E0_F6C3B757_E900.png).
- `education/nanjing-agricultural-university.png`: official emblem from the [university's motto and emblem page](https://www.njau.edu.cn/xx/list.htm); [original image](https://www.njau.edu.cn/_upload/article/images/a7/a1/27c696bb42d7a2807782f8778ec7/9ff0825f-2669-4316-92b0-37b7cd9b6ca2.png).
- `experience/bytedance.png`: original 300 × 300 transparent brand symbol embedded in the favicon of the [ByteDance official homepage](https://www.bytedance.com/en/).
- `experience/seer.png`: original transparent SEER ROBOTICS wordmark extracted without visual changes from the PNG embedded in the [official SVG](https://cdn1.seer-group.com/static/en/common/en-logo.svg), linked by the [SEER Robotics homepage](https://seer-robotics.com/).
- `experience/everwise.jpg`: [original company-directory image](https://innohere.oss-cn-shenzhen.aliyuncs.com/images/company/2025/12/08/6e621acb9c0003cdfb45696e8b21676a.jpg), visually cross-checked against the company's orange-and-black logo in [funding announcement materials credited to 知有无界EverWise](https://media.qimingpian.cn/2026/01/08/6b77837000af10a104853f733077e1a3.html). The company website was unavailable when checked. The original image is unchanged; CSS reduces its outer whitespace for display.
- `experience/china-mobile.svg`: the five original symbol paths and colors extracted from the [official China Mobile logo SVG](https://www.chinamobileltd.com/en/images/global/logo.svg), linked by its [corporate homepage](https://www.chinamobileltd.com/en/global/home.php). The symbol represents the China Mobile partner of the joint institute; it is not presented as a separate joint-institute emblem.
- `publications/arfh-framework.png`: cropped from Figure 1, page 3 of the owner's local manuscript, *Adaptive Role Flipping Based Robust Cross-Modal Hashing*. The figure is linked to the [publisher's page](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-92-2497-5_32).

Sources accessed on 23 October 2026. The MIT template license does not apply to university emblems, company logos, or the paper figure. Company marks retain their original colors and proportions and identify the organizations listed in the owner's experience.

The `.webp` alternatives for these three PNGs use lossless encoding at the original dimensions. Decoded color and alpha values are identical; the agricultural university emblem's XMP metadata is retained. The page uses `<picture>` to select WebP, while the original PNGs remain available as fallbacks and direct image links. The profile avatar remains JPEG.
