    My research interests lie in robust multimodal learning and robot perception, particularly cross-modal retrieval with noisy labels and visual recognition for agricultural inspection.

    I am a master's student in Control Science and Intelligent Engineering at Nanjing University. In 2025, I completed my undergraduate studies in Automation at Nanjing Agricultural University.

    🔥 Recent News

    • Jul. 2026: Our paper Adaptive Role Flipping Based Robust Cross-Modal Hashing was published online in the FLINS-ISKE 2026 proceedings.
    • Jul. 2025: Our paper DualHet-YOLO was published in Agriculture.

    🔬 Research Interests

    • Robust Cross-modal Retrieval: Representation and retrieval across modalities, with a focus on how label noise affects learning.
    • Visual Perception for Agricultural Inspection: Lightweight object detection for agricultural inspection robots and animal behavior recognition.
